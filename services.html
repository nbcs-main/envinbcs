Core Service Areas

Our service areas are organized around the actual needs of projects: understanding the site, securing the right requirements, designing workable solutions, monitoring performance, and coordinating with stakeholders. Depending on the project, NBCS can provide focused technical support or assemble a multi-disciplinary team for broader implementation.

Integrated Project Support

From early planning to implementation

Many project issues do not fit neatly into one discipline. A water supply concern may involve hydrogeology, monitoring, permitting, public consultation, and engineering. A waste facility may require characterization, design, regulatory coordination, environmental monitoring, and stakeholder communication. NBCS helps connect those requirements so project teams can make better decisions earlier.

Regulatory, Technical, and Stakeholder Coordination

Environmental and engineering projects often require coordination across multiple groups. NBCS assists clients in preparing technical inputs, organizing documentation, clarifying agency-facing requirements, and supporting communication with communities or stakeholders when applicable.

  • Information and Education Campaigns
  • Public scoping and public consultation support
  • Conflict resolution and project management consultancy
  • Environmental and conservation education
  • Regulatory coordination and documentation support
  • Retainership for ongoing compliance coordination and documentation support
  • Environmental monitoring and laboratory coordination
  • Technical specialist coordination
  • Project implementation follow-through

Note: The exact scope, documents, studies, permits, and technical team members depend on project type, location, scale, regulatory coverage, site conditions, and client objectives. NBCS can help review your project context and identify the practical next steps.
