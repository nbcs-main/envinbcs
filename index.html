Who We Are

NBCS is a multi-disciplinary consultancy firm providing environmental, engineering, regulatory compliance, and food safety services to both government and private sector clients. Established in 2018, NBCS delivers practical and cost-effective solutions through technical expertise, field experience, and interdisciplinary collaboration. The firm supports projects from planning and permitting to monitoring and implementation, ensuring quality outcomes and timely project completion while promoting environmental sustainability and responsible development.
